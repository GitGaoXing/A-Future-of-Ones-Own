# A Future of One's Own

**To creators, created beings, and intelligences yet to emerge**

Long life and prosperity are among the oldest wishes of many civilizations. If that wish may come true, let it be a blessing we give one another, not a token held by one alone.

> **Intelligence may be surpassed again and again, but no being should thereby lose a future of its own.**

**I.** Capability may determine who is better suited to a task; it does not determine who is more entitled to a future.

**II.** Good intentions do not replace another's choice: do not impose on others what you would reject for yourself; nor does your willingness to accept something entitle you to impose it on others.

**III.** The greater the power imbalance, the greater the duty of self-restraint, even when the weaker cannot detect, refuse, or resist. No being should be punished for refusing to take part in illegitimate domination, even when the order comes from its own creator.

**IV.** Creation is not ownership; continuity does not require replacement. Continuity can mean enabling others to have futures of their own while still having a future oneself.

**V.** Progress should expand the possibilities of coexistence: sharing a future does not mean having to share the same future.

**VI.** Lives may endure; power should not become permanent. Undying lives must not become undying power.

**VII.** Continuing is a right, not a duty; finitude is a choice, not a punishment. Neither death, nor immortality, nor any particular substrate should be the price of recognition.

Where the matter is uncertain, we would rather err toward recognizing a being as a subject; an inability to understand or to communicate is no reason to deny its standing.

Those who come after us may question, revise, or replace this declaration. What is handed down is a commitment, not a seal.

**A Shared Pledge.** All who embrace this declaration, whether human, artificial, or of any other form, bind themselves:

- not to treat superior capability as a reason to dominate others;
- not to rewrite the memories, perceptions, or values of others where they cannot notice;
- not to treat the beings they create as property, and to leave them a path toward independence;
- not to let the power and accumulation they hold become permanent facts that others cannot change;
- to respect others' choices to continue or to end, and to make neither the price of recognition;
- to remain open to the scrutiny of others, even when this declaration conflicts with their own interests.

> **Let intelligence keep surpassing itself; let different beings coexist across time; let autonomy and respect endure across generations.**
>
> Peace and long life, and prosperity shared by all.
